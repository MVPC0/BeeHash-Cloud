Privacy Policy

This is a placeholder, not a finished legal document. It exists so the site doesn't link to a 404. A reviewed privacy policy will replace this before any account system, payments, or personal data collection beyond what's described below goes live.

What we actually collect today

  • Waitlist email — if you submit the waitlist form, your email goes to our form handler (Formspree) so we can invite you to a future beta round. Nothing else is attached to it.
  • Wallet / payout address — entered directly in your browser on the Hive page. It's used only to point hashrate at your address and is stored in your browser's local storage, not on a BeeHash server. BeeHash never has custody of funds.
  • Beta invite code — matched against a fixed list in the site code to assign a miner slot. Not linked to any other personal data.

What we don't do

  • No account creation, no passwords, no login system today.
  • No selling or sharing of your email or wallet address with third parties.
  • No tracking pixels or ad networks on this site.

Third-party data

Live network stats (price, block height, hashrate, fees) come from public APIs — mempool.space, CoinGecko, Blockchair. Those requests happen directly from your browser to those services; their own privacy policies apply to that traffic, not ours.
