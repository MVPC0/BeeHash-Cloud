Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

What we collect

  • Waitlist email — if you submit the form (via Formspree or similar).
  • Browser storage — Hive may store your chain choice and wallet address locally on your device for the demo session (not a full account database).
  • Basic technical data — standard web logs may be processed by GitHub Pages / CDN (IP, user agent) under their policies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your email list.
  • We do not ask for private keys or seed phrases. Never enter those on BeeHash.
  • We do not custody your coins.

Payout addresses

If you enter a BTC or BCH address in the Hive, it is used as a payout / worker identity for solo mining destinations. Treat it as public information (as on any pool).

Third parties

Formspree (waitlist), GitHub Pages (hosting), and public APIs (e.g. mempool.space) process data under their own policies when you use those features.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@beehash.cloud
