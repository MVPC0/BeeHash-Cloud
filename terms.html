What BeeHash Cloud is, today

BeeHash provides SHA-256 hashrate hosting. During beta, seats are invite-only, free, and limited to ~45–50 TH/s for 7 days. You supply your own BTC or BCH payout address. Solo pool payouts go directly to that address on-chain — BeeHash never takes custody of coins and cannot intercept a payout.

No guarantees
