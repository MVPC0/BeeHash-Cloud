Ports — rental vs normal solo
Separate lanes on purpose. Normal solo-miner ports are for people pointing their own machines. Rental delivery uses a dedicated path so rented TH is metered, routed, and checked without mixing into hobby solo traffic.

How rental delivery works

Choose TH/s and term

Pick a hashrate size and duration. Capacity is capped by real fleet size — BeeHash will not sell more TH than public proof supports.

Set destination

Preset solo/shared pool or custom stratum + worker + password. Automatic routing binds your order to that target.

Pay → hash routes

After payment confirmation, rented power is delivered on the rental lane and routed to your destination. You do not reconfigure physical ASICs.

Live delivery checks

Monitor effective hashrate and share acceptance toward your target. Cross-check on the destination pool’s public page when available.
